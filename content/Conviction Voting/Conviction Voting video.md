[[Conviction voting]] is the first "real time" voting, or preference streaming.

How can this be used? What if every community member could signal what it was that was important to them?  Like fixing potholes, or supporting black lives matter movement.

What if that signal could be aggregated and visible to policy makers?  Like a continuous petition, that actually worked.

How would these people get these Voting tokens? Well you could hand them out democratically.  You could allow people to volunteer in their communities to earn more. You could allow people to fund public goods projects and receive them in exchange.
